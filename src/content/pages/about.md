Hi, I'm Rayen. This is where I keep my technical notes: things I've built, broken, debugged, and eventually understood.

The name _Stray Gradients_ is a nod to the loose ends of deep learning work: the gradients that go somewhere unexpected, and the side paths that turn out to be worth writing down.

## What I'm interested in

- **Large-scale training.** How transformer models are trained across many GPUs, and the systems details that decide whether a run is fast, stable, or neither.
- **Reinforcement learning for language models.** RLHF and its relatives (PPO, GRPO, REINFORCE-style methods), and the infrastructure that makes them scale.
- **Agentic coding tools.** How coding agents work, where they help, and where they fall over.
